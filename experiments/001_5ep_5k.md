# Experiment 001

## Dataset

- Episodes: 5
- Frames: 4,455
- Cameras: top + side
- Resolution: 640x480
- FPS: 30

Dataset:
mikami235/so101_pick_place_5ep_01

## Training

Policy: ACT
Steps: 5,000
Batch size: 8
GPU: Tesla T4

Model:
mikami235/act_so101_pick_place_5ep_01

## Result

Training completed successfully.

Final training log:

- loss: ~0.30
- l1_loss: ~0.11
- kld_loss: ~0.019

The policy could be loaded and executed on the real SO-101.

However, the robot mainly moved toward / around the initial pose
and did not successfully reach the pick operation.

## Observation

Possible causes:

- Too few episodes (5)
- Too few training steps (5k)
- Limited trajectory diversity
- CPU inference slower than target 30 Hz

CPU rollout typically ran around 15–29 Hz,
with occasional large inference latency.
